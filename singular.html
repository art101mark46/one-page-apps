How to Play

Game Rules

The Cards

Every card shows three things: a colour, a number (1–8) or action icon, and a symbol shape in its corners.

Matching — any ONE is enough
  • 🎨 Colour — play any card the same colour as the top card.
  • 🔢 Number — or any card showing the same number.
  • ⭐ Symbol — or any card showing the same symbol shape.

Nothing matches? Draw a card from the deck — the next player continues.

Action Cards & Wild
  • 🚫 Skip — the next player misses a turn.
  • ➕2 Draw Two — the next player draws two cards and is skipped.
  • 🔄 Reverse — the turn order flips direction.
  • 🌈 Wild — you choose the new colour; only that colour (or another wild) can follow.
  • 📣 SINGULAR! — declare when you're down to your last card, or draw a penalty card.
Match Badge

The badge under the discard pile lists every legal match for the top card — COLOUR • NUMBER • SYMBOL. You only need ONE of them. After a wild card it reads COLOUR ONLY.
